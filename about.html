About

Every artwork tells a story.

Heer Art Gallery is a one-person studio making mixed media paintings and nature-formed wall art. Each piece starts as a sketch or a walk outside, and is built up slowly by hand — nothing here is printed, and no two pieces are quite the same.

The work moves between devotional portraits, quiet landscapes, and sculptural pieces made from twine, wire and dried botanicals. What ties it together is a slow, handmade process and a habit of noticing small things worth keeping.
